# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to keeping an open, respectful and welcoming environment for
all participants, regardless of experience level, background, identity or
first language.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Being respectful, direct and specific — about code, not about people.
- Accepting technical criticism, and giving it with the reasoning attached.
- Assuming the other person is trying to help, especially across a language
  barrier.

Not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or issues.

## Scope

This applies to everything in this repository — issues, pull requests,
discussions and commit messages — and to anywhere someone is representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer, privately, through
[GitHub](https://github.com/NullSablex). Reports are handled discreetly.
Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and
may block a participant who keeps doing so.

This document is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
